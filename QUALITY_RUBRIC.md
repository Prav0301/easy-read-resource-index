# Accessible Resource Quality Rubric

## Purpose

This rubric provides a structured framework for reviewing accessible information resources before inclusion or archival.

The aim is not to create rigid pass/fail criteria, but to support:

- consistent curation
- transparent review
- neurodevelopmental accessibility
- CAMHS suitability
- communication quality
- clinical usability

---

# Review domains

## 1. Accessibility quality

Evaluate:

- sentence simplicity
- visual clarity
- layout readability
- chunking of information
- symbol/picture support
- low cognitive load
- readability for learning disability populations

### Suggested ratings

| Score | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| 1 | Poor accessibility |
| 2 | Limited accessibility |
| 3 | Reasonably accessible |
| 4 | Strong accessible design |
| 5 | Excellent Easy Read / accessible communication |

---

## 2. Neurodevelopmental suitability

Evaluate:

- sensory friendliness
- predictability of layout
- concrete language
- reduced ambiguity
- autism suitability
- ADHD suitability
- suitability for intellectual disability

---

## 3. CAMHS suitability

Evaluate:

- adolescent relevance
- developmental appropriateness
- family usefulness
- emotional tone
- applicability to CAMHS workflows

Important:
Adult-oriented imagery does not automatically exclude a resource if clinically adaptable.

---

## 4. Medication communication quality

For psychopharmacology resources evaluate:

- side-effect explanations
- balanced communication
- shared decision-making support
- monitoring explanations
- non-coercive tone
- suitability for supported consent discussions

---

## 5. Rights and legal accessibility

Evaluate:

- explanation of rights
- procedural transparency
- readability of legal concepts
- Mental Health Act accessibility
- advocacy explanations

---

## 6. Clinical utility

Evaluate:

- likely real-world reuse
- inpatient usefulness
- clinic usefulness
- psychoeducation usefulness
- crisis usefulness
- communication-support value

---

# Recommended metadata outputs

Following review, resources may receive:

- Featured status
- Verification status
- Clinical notes
- Developmental notes
- Recommended use annotations
- Archive priority

---

# Suggested qualitative labels

Optional qualitative labels:

- Gold standard
- High utility
- Adaptable for CAMHS
- Parent-facing
- Young-person-facing
- LD-focused
- Neurodevelopmentally friendly
- Rights-focused
- Medication-focused
- Inpatient useful

---

# Exclusion considerations

Resources may be excluded if they are:

- inaccessible despite claiming Easy Read status
- visually overwhelming
- outdated
- inaccurate
- strongly adult-specific without adaptation value
- impossible to verify
- heavily duplicated
- low clinical utility

---

# Important principle

Clinical usefulness and communication usefulness are not always identical.

Some highly useful clinical resources:

- are not strict Easy Read
- use adult imagery
- require supported reading
- work best alongside clinician explanation

This repository intentionally preserves nuanced judgement rather than rigid binary inclusion rules.
