Independent resource index. Not official NHS guidance. Check local clinical suitability, copyright, and review dates before use.

Scope: This directory is a signposting tool for clinicians. Visit the source sites to identify resources suitable for sharing with young people, families, and carers in your care. A future version may be adapted for direct public access; the current version is not designed for that purpose.

⚠ Not medical advice. This directory does not constitute medical advice and does not endorse the clinical content of any linked resource. Clinicians remain responsible for assessing the suitability, currency, and accuracy of any resource before sharing it with a patient or family. Always check local trust policies, the resource's review date, and clinical fit for the individual.
